We started by
rebuilding our own.

This is an internal product case study: the evolution of software we use and the foundation behind our industry-specific billing systems.

Before offering billing software to other businesses, we wanted a stronger foundation in our own.

The starting point was a simple desktop application built with Python and Tkinter. We rewrote it into a more complete billing and management product, bringing together GST-ready invoicing, inventory tracking and clear daily reporting.

Using the product internally gives us direct experience of everyday billing friction. That experience informs the systems we deliver and the questions we ask when discussing a client’s counter, stock and reporting workflow.

Product principle

Keep the core dependable. Adapt the workflow to the business using it.

Three everyday needs.
One product foundation.

The rebuild focused on the core activities businesses return to each day: billing, stock and reporting.

01 / Invoice₹

GST-ready invoicing

Invoicing became part of a broader business management product. The rebuilt application supports GST-ready billing as a core capability, giving industry-specific systems a common foundation.

  • Business invoice creation
  • GST-ready billing capability
  • A shared invoicing core
02 / Track▦

Inventory tracking

The product brings stock tracking alongside billing. Inventory is part of the same product story, rather than an afterthought to the invoice screen.

  • Inventory tracking built in
  • A clearer view of stock
  • A foundation for industry workflows
03 / Review≡

Clear daily reporting

Daily reporting supports a clearer view of business activity. Together with invoicing and inventory, it helps turn an earlier billing tool into a more useful management product.

  • Day-to-day business summaries
  • Reporting within the core product
  • Information alongside billing and stock

The same core.
Different counters.

The core engine is adapted into the sixteen industry-specific billing systems described on this site.

A salon, a clinic, a jewellery shop and a garage do not work in exactly the same way. The rebuild provided a reusable foundation that could be adapted to those different business contexts.

The shared core covers billing, inventory and reporting. The industry-specific systems build on that foundation around the workflow of the business, rather than treating every counter as identical.
