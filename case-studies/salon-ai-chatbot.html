The starting point

Great service deserves
a better first conversation.

This story covers website and chatbot work across salon clients, including Infinity Unisex Salon and The 7S Family Salon.

When the team is looking after a customer, the next enquiry can be easy to miss.

Salon staff balance appointments, walk-ins and phone calls throughout the day. Questions about services and prices often repeat, while customers browsing after hours still need a useful way to get information.

We rebuilt the website experience around mobile visitors and added MR.COOL, a reusable chatbot widget deployed across salon and beauty clients. The aim was to make the next step clearer: discover services, ask a question and move toward an appointment conversation.

Design principle

Make the website welcoming. Make answers easy to find. Keep the next step obvious.

What we delivered

A website that welcomes.
An assistant that helps.

Three connected parts support the journey from an online visit to a booking enquiry.
