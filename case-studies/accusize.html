The starting point

More than a
brochure website.

The challenge was both public-facing and operational: present the business clearly, while supporting the processes that keep it running.

Accusize’s products depend on precision. Its business systems needed the same attention to detail.

As a precision gauge manufacturer, the company handles raw materials, production stages, quality checks and calibration records. These activities had outgrown spreadsheet tracking and called for a system built around the actual manufacturing workflow.

The project also needed to address how buyers encounter the business online. A production-ready website would present the catalogue and company credibility, while an internal platform would bring inventory, production and billing together.

Design principle

Give buyers a clear view of the business. Give the team a connected view of its operations.

What we delivered

Three parts.
A more coherent system.

Each part addresses a different need, from the first buyer impression to the records used inside the business.

A professional public website

A production website presenting Accusize’s catalogue and credibility clearly to prospective buyers. The public-facing experience gives customers a structured introduction to the manufacturer and its products.

  • A professional buyer-facing presence
