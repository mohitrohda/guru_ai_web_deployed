Accusize Gauges & Tools

A Pune precision gauge manufacturer needed a professional public presence and an internal system built around its operations.

The starting point

Raw materials, production stages, quality checks and calibration records had outgrown spreadsheet tracking. A brochure website alone would not address that operational complexity.

What we built

We delivered a production website alongside an internal ERP connecting inventory, production and billing. A dedicated calibration module brought instrument history into the business workflow.

  • Dedicated instrument calibration records

A practical project conversation

Start with your workflow.
Then choose the technology.

You do not need a complete technical brief to start. These four questions help turn a business need into a useful first discussion.

  1. 01 / Context

    What happens today?

    Describe the process, the people involved and where work gets delayed or repeated.

  2. 02 / Priority

    What matters first?

    Identify the customer experience or daily task that would benefit most from improvement.

  3. 03 / Scope

    What must connect?

    List the tools, records and communication channels your system needs to work with.

  4. 04 / Delivery

    What comes next?

    Discuss scope, a suitable delivery plan and the support you will need after launch.
