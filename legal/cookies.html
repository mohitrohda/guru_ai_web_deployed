Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

Our approach

We keep this website intentionally light. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run third-party analytics scripts that profile you.

What may be set

  • Essential platform items, our hosting and security provider (Cloudflare) may set strictly necessary items to protect the site from abuse and deliver it efficiently.
  • Font delivery, fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives standard technical request data (such as your IP address) to serve the font files.

The GURU chat assistant runs entirely in your browser during your visit and does not set tracking cookies.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Because we don't rely on non-essential cookies, blocking them will not break this website.

Changes

If we ever introduce analytics or other cookies, this page will be updated first, and consent will be requested where required.
