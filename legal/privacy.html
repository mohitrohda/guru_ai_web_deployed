Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

Who we are

GURU AI TECHNOLOGIES ("we", "us") is an AI engineering studio based in Pune, Maharashtra, India. This policy explains what information we collect through guruaitechnologies.com and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Enquiry details you send us, name, phone number, email address, the service you're interested in, and your message, when you submit the contact form or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Chat messages, what you type to the GURU assistant on this site. These messages are processed in your browser during your visit and are not stored on our servers.
  • Basic technical data, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may log IP addresses and request data to serve the site securely and prevent abuse.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare proposals and provide the services you request.
  • To maintain the security and performance of this website.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

GURU AI assistant and project enquiries

The GURU AI chat assistant is an automated assistant. Messages you type may be sent to an AI service provider to generate a reply, so please do not enter passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. We do not keep your chat conversation after you close the chat.

If you ask the assistant to start a project enquiry, it collects your name, email, phone, service interest and project details, shows you a summary, and stores them only after you give explicit consent. Stored enquiries are used only to contact you about your request. You can ask us to correct or delete them at contact@guruaitechnologies.com. Our team may reply by email or WhatsApp.

Sharing

Your details are shared only with service providers that make this website work, such as our hosting platform and, if you use the contact form, the form delivery service, and only to the extent needed to deliver your message to us. We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to handle your request and any project that follows, after which they are deleted from our records on request or when no longer required.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. Email info@guruaitechnologies.com and we will act on your request promptly, in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Third-party links

This site links to external services such as WhatsApp. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above always reflects the current version.
