Common questions

How much does a website or software project cost?

Every business is different, so we don't publish fixed prices. Share your requirements on WhatsApp or through the form below and we'll send a clear, custom quote, usually within 24 hours.

How long does a typical project take?

A premium business website usually takes 1 to 3 weeks. Custom software, apps, and automation projects are scoped individually, you'll get a realistic timeline before we start, and honest updates throughout.

Will my website work well on mobile?

Yes, we design mobile-first. Every project is tested on real phone, tablet, and desktop sizes with no broken layouts, no horizontal scrolling, and fast loading even on 4G.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes post-launch support, and we offer ongoing maintenance plans for updates, backups, and improvements. You're never left alone with your product.

Can you add AI to my existing website or business?

Absolutely. We can add chatbots, automation, or smart features to what you already have, you don't need to rebuild everything to benefit from AI.

Where are you located and do you work remotely?

We're based in Pune, Maharashtra, and work with clients across India and internationally. We're remote-first by design, calls are scheduled around your time zone, updates happen in writing so nothing waits on a meeting, and you can preview your project live from anywhere in the world.
