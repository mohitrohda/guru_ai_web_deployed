01 / Our story

Good software starts with understanding the business.

From freelance web development in Pune to a studio building connected business systems.

A salon booking an appointment. A manufacturer preparing a quotation. A retailer tracking stock. Different businesses, with the same need: tools that make the day run better.

GURU AI TECHNOLOGIES began with web development and grew into custom software, billing systems, AI chatbots, and automation. Our focus remains the businesses behind the brief: salons, clinics, retail shops, manufacturers, and local service providers.

That work includes an internal ERP for a precision manufacturer and a reusable AI chatbot used across salon clients. Each project helps us understand the details that matter when software becomes part of someone’s everyday work.

Built here. Delivered wherever you work.

Our GST-focused billing software serves Indian businesses. Websites, custom software, chatbots, and automation are delivered remotely for clients in India and internationally.
