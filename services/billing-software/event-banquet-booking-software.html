Overview

What this means for your business

A banquet hall only has so many dates, and double booking one is a costly mistake. We build booking and billing software for event venues that shows a clear calendar of every date, tracks advance payments against each booking, and generates a final bill based on guest count and services chosen.

Catering, decoration, and hall charges can be billed together, with advances automatically adjusted, so the final settlement matches what was actually agreed with the client.

In practice

Follow a venue booking from enquiry to final bill

A venue might take an advance months before an event, then adjust the bill after the final guest count is confirmed. One booking record can keep the event date, agreed package, and payment history together.

See availability

A calendar can show enquiries, held dates, and confirmed bookings. We agree how staff should handle a hold that has not become a booking.

Track advances and packages

Record the agreed package and any payments already received against the event.

Prepare the closing bill

When the guest count or extras change, staff can review those details and the advances before asking for the remaining amount.

What to share with us

Bring your booking calendar, a package sheet, and an example where the guest count or price changed.

FAQ

Questions we hear about this service

Can it prevent double booking of the same date?

Yes. The booking calendar shows real time availability, so a date can't be booked twice by mistake.

Can advance payments be tracked separately from the final bill?

Yes. Advances are recorded against each booking and adjusted automatically in the final invoice.

Can billing be based on final guest count?

Yes. Catering and service charges can be billed according to the confirmed guest count.
