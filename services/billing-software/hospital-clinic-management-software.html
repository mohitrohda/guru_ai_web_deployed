What this means for your business

Clinics juggle appointments, patient history, and billing at the same time, often with one receptionist managing all three. We build clinic management software that keeps these connected: a patient's appointment, visit notes, and bill all live in one record instead of three different registers.

Access is role based, so sensitive patient information stays visible only to the staff who need it. Billing supports consultation fees, procedures, and pharmacy or lab charges together, with reports your accountant can use directly.

See how a visit moves through the front desk

A patient books an appointment, checks in, and pays for the agreed services. We can connect the administrative steps so front desk staff can find the visit, check its status, and issue the bill from the correct record.

Book and find an appointment

Set up appointment types and schedules around the way your front desk and practitioners work.

Separate access by role

We agree which information reception, billing staff, and authorised practitioners need to see. Sensitive records can be restricted to the agreed roles.

Keep charges with the visit

Staff can review the service items, payment, and receipt for a particular visit without searching separate registers.

What to share with us

Bring sample visit and billing forms with personal details removed. Tell us who uses each screen and which information they need.

Capabilities that come standard

Appointment Scheduling

A clear daily and weekly calendar for doctors, with reminders to cut down no-shows.

Patient Records

Visit history, prescriptions, and notes kept in one searchable, access controlled record.

Consultation & Procedure Billing

Bill consultations, procedures, and pharmacy items together in a single invoice.

Role Based Access

Doctors, receptionists, and admin staff each see only what they need to.

Daily Collection Reports

A clear view of cash, card, and UPI collections at the end of each day.

Data Privacy Controls

What your business gains

  • Billing errors reduced with pre set consultation rates
  • Clear daily collection summaries for the owner
  • Fewer missed appointments with automatic reminders

Questions we hear about this service

Is patient data kept private and secure?

Yes. Access is role based and every record is permission controlled, so only authorised staff can view sensitive details.

Can it handle multiple doctors and schedules?

Yes. Each doctor gets their own calendar, and the front desk can manage all of them from one screen.

Can it bill for procedures and pharmacy items together?

Yes. Consultation fees, procedures, and pharmacy or lab charges can all be combined into a single clean invoice.
