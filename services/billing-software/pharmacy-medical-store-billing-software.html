Overview

What this means for your business

A pharmacy cannot afford billing mistakes. Medicines need to be tracked by batch and expiry date, not just by name, and stock has to be monitored closely enough that nothing expires unnoticed on a shelf. We build pharmacy billing software around exactly this requirement.

Every bill is linked to the correct batch, expiry alerts arrive well before a problem, and low stock warnings mean a customer is never turned away for something that should have been reordered days earlier.

In practice

See which stock batch was sold

Two packs of the same medicine may have different batch numbers and expiry dates. When staff select a product for a sale, they can see the batch they are recording and check the dates before completing the bill.

Record batches at receipt

We can record the batch, expiry, and quantity when stock comes in so each group can be identified.

Show batch details at the counter

Staff can check the batch on the pack against the one selected for the sale. The bill can update the corresponding stock record.

Review stock that needs attention

Reports can list items close to expiry and those that have reached an agreed low-stock level.

What to share with us

Bring a sample purchase bill and examples of items that are sold from more than one batch. Explain how staff handle expired stock and returns.
