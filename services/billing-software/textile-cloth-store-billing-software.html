What this means for your business

Cloth stores sell by the metre, by size, and by variant, which general retail billing software often handles badly. We built this understanding into Rangrez, a premium cloth and fashion store site and system for the Maharashtra market.

Our textile billing software tracks fabric rolls, sizes, and colour variants as separate stock items, bills by metre or by piece depending on the product, and keeps your inventory accurate across a wide, fast moving catalogue.

Record stock in the units you sell

A textile shop may buy a roll and sell pieces by the metre, or sell garments by size and colour. We can set up items and units so staff know what they are billing and what quantity should remain afterwards.

Separate rolls, pieces, and variants

Set up the categories, sizes, and colours that customers ask for, along with the units your staff use.

Record the quantity sold

The bill can record a measured length or a number of pieces. We agree how to record offcuts or adjustments.

Check what is still available

Stock views can show the remaining quantity and help staff find the item or variant when the next customer asks for it.

What to share with us

Bring a sample item list, a bill that uses different units, and an example of a stock adjustment.

Capabilities that come standard

Variant & Size Tracking

Track stock by size, colour, and fabric variant as distinct, countable items.

Billing by Metre or Piece

Bill fabric by the metre and finished garments by the piece, in the same system.

Season & Collection Tags

Organise stock by season or collection for faster counter lookups.

Wholesale & Retail Pricing

Set different pricing tiers for wholesale buyers and walk in retail customers.

Stock Ageing Reports

See which fabric or stock has been sitting the longest, for better clearance decisions.

GST Ready Billing

Correct GST handling on both fabric and garments, formatted for your accountant.
