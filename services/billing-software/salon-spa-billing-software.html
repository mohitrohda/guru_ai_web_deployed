Overview

What this means for your business

A salon counter rarely has a spare minute. Between appointments, product sales, and staff commission, billing has to be fast and correct the first time. We build salon billing software that lets any stylist or receptionist create a bill in seconds, with the right GST applied and the service list already matched to your menu.

It tracks package balances (so a client's remaining sessions are always visible), calculates staff commission automatically, and keeps product inventory synced with every sale. Owners get a clear daily report without chasing anyone for numbers.

In practice

Follow a client visit and the package it uses

A client might pay for a package up front and use one session on each visit. Front desk staff can check the remaining sessions, record the treatment used, and see if anything needs to be billed separately.

Set up services and packages

We can record service prices, package contents, and the number of visits that should be included.

Track visits and balances

When staff record a session, the client's package history can show what was used and what is still available.

Connect staff and sales records

Depending on your rules, we can record which staff member delivered a service and organise the information for commission review.

What to share with us

Bring a service menu, package rules, and examples of how staff record a walk-in and a package visit.

FAQ

Questions we hear about this service

Can it track prepaid packages and memberships?

Yes. Package balances update automatically with every visit, and the system flags when a client is close to running out.

Does it calculate staff commission?

Yes. Commission rules can be set per stylist or per service, and totals are ready for payday without manual tracking.

Will it work for a multi-chair salon with several staff billing at once?

Yes. Multiple counters can bill at the same time with role based access for staff, managers, and owners.
