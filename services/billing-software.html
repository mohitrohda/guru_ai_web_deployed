Overview

What this service really means for you

Most billing software is built for accountants. Yours should be built for your counter: fast invoice entry, obvious buttons, GST handled correctly without anyone thinking about it, and stock that updates itself. That's what we build, billing software designed for real Indian shops and service businesses.

Daily summaries land automatically. GST reports are one click. Inventory warns you before you run out. And because it's built by us, it can grow with you, new features, new reports, new integrations, without switching products or losing data.

In practice

What happens between a sale and the daily report

At a shop counter, staff choose items, record how the customer paid, and give them an invoice. The same transaction can update stock and appear in the owner's daily summary, so the team spends less time entering it again later.

Set up the items you sell

We can set up products, prices, tax settings, and starting stock. If you have an existing list, we can review its format before import.

Handle everyday exceptions

A return, discount, split payment, or unpaid bill should be recorded clearly. We map the cases your staff actually handle at the counter.

Read the totals at closing

Set up sales, stock, and payment views that let a manager trace a total back to the transactions that created it.

What to share with us

Bring a sample invoice, an item list, and the reports you use now. Include a return or adjustment if those are common. Compare billing and accounting software →
