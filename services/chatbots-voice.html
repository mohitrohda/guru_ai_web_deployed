Overview

What this service really means for you

Customers ask questions at 11 PM. They message on Sundays. They want prices, timings, and directions right now, and if they don't get answers, they message your competitor. Our AI chatbots answer instantly, around the clock, in the languages your customers actually use: English, Hinglish, and Roman Marathi.

We build assistants for websites and WhatsApp that greet visitors, answer FAQs, qualify leads, and hand over to a human exactly when it matters. Architecture is future-proof: start with a fast rule-based brain today, upgrade to advanced AI models tomorrow, without rebuilding anything.

In practice

How an assistant can handle a customer enquiry

A customer might ask about prices, hours, or a booking when staff are busy. An assistant can use information you approve, collect the details needed to help, and pass the conversation to a person when it needs judgment.

Choose what it should know

We organise common questions and answers from your own information. You decide which subjects the assistant can handle.

Set the conversation steps

We can build a flow for common actions such as collecting contact details, taking a booking request, or directing someone to the right service.

Make handover clear

We define when to ask a follow-up question and when to pass the enquiry to your team. The handover can include the context already provided.

What to share with us

Bring examples of real enquiries, the languages your customers use, and the questions that should always go to a person.

FAQ

Questions we hear about this service

Does it work on WhatsApp too?

Yes, we build for website chat, WhatsApp, or both. WhatsApp integration uses official APIs for reliability.

What happens when the bot doesn't know an answer?

It says so honestly and routes the customer to you on WhatsApp, no dead ends, no made-up answers.

Can it really understand Marathi and Hinglish?

Yes, it detects Roman-script Marathi and Hinglish automatically and replies in the same language. Try the assistant on this site!

Can we update what it knows?

Yes, the knowledge base is modular and editable. Add services, change timings, or expand answers anytime.
