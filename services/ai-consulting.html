Overview

What this service really means for you

AI is powerful, and heavily oversold. The difference between an AI investment that pays for itself and one that becomes expensive decoration is knowing exactly where it fits your business. That's what AI Consulting delivers: an honest, prioritized roadmap grounded in your real workflows.

We audit how your business actually operates, identify where AI creates measurable value, automation, customer service, insights, and tell you plainly where it doesn't. You leave with a practical plan: what to build first, what it should cost, and what results to expect. No jargon. No hype tax.

In practice

What you get from an AI planning project

If your team spends a lot of time answering the same questions, there are several possible fixes. A clearer help page, a simple rule, and an AI assistant each solve a different problem. We help you choose based on the actual work, data, and effort involved.

A map of the work

We discuss where time is lost, which tools staff already use, and which decisions need human judgment.

A short list of options

We compare possible improvements, including what they depend on and what would make them hard to maintain. You can see which idea is worth trying first.

A test you can review

If a small trial makes sense, we define the sample inputs and the results you would check before spending more.

What to share with us

Share the business problem in your own words, who does the work, and how you know when it goes well. Technical specifications can come later.

FAQ

Questions we hear about this service

What does a consulting engagement look like?

Typically: a discovery call, a workflow audit, then a written roadmap presented and discussed, from days to a few weeks depending on business size.

We're a small business, is AI even relevant?

Often yes, in unglamorous ways: automated follow-ups, faster quotes, a chatbot for repeated questions. Small businesses frequently see the fastest payback.

Will you just recommend building things with you?

No, the roadmap stands on its own and you can take it anywhere. Many clients do build with us, but the advice is honest either way.

What if we already started an AI project?

We can review it, architecture, costs, direction, and either validate it or course-correct before more budget is spent.
