What this service really means for you

Small businesses are targeted precisely because attackers assume they're unprotected, automated attacks don't check your company size. Cyber Security Solutions makes you a hard target: hardened websites, audited code, secure forms, and infrastructure configured to resist the attacks that hit every public site daily.

We secure what you have and build securely from the start: security headers, input sanitization, dependency audits, HTTPS-everywhere, and sensible access control. Clear reports tell you what was found, what was fixed, and what to watch, in language that doesn't require a security degree.

What we check when reviewing a website or application

A review can uncover places where people have more access than they need, a setting exposes information, or a missed update leaves a known weakness. We agree what can be tested before starting and report what we find in language your team can use.

Review the parts in scope

We can examine agreed areas such as sign-in, permissions, public settings, and software updates. We document what we tested.

Explain what to fix first

Each finding describes the affected area, what could happen, and a practical next step. This helps your team plan work by impact.

Check the fixes

If the scope includes repair or follow-up, we can review the relevant changes and record the result so your team can see what was resolved.

What to share with us

Tell us which sites or systems are yours to authorise for review, who manages them, and what changes or concerns prompted the check.

Questions we hear about this service

We're small, would anyone really attack us?

Attacks are automated and indiscriminate: bots probe every public website daily. Small businesses are hit constantly precisely because defenses are assumed weak.

What does a security audit include?

Headers, HTTPS configuration, form and input handling, dependency vulnerabilities, and information leakage, delivered as a prioritized, plain-language report.

Can you secure a website another developer built?

Yes, audits and hardening on existing sites are among our most common security engagements. No blame, just fixes.

Is one audit enough forever?

Security is a posture, not a purchase. One audit fixes today's issues; periodic reviews keep you protected as your site and threats evolve.
