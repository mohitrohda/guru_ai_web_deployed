Overview

What this service really means for you

Behind every fast, reliable product is boring, excellent infrastructure. We set up modern cloud foundations, Cloudflare Pages and Workers, automated CI/CD pipelines, monitoring, and backups, so your product stays online, loads instantly worldwide, and updates with a single push.

If your current site is slow, your hosting bills are mysterious, or deployments involve crossed fingers, this is the fix. We migrate carefully, automate ruthlessly, and document everything, leaving you with infrastructure you understand and costs that often round to zero.

In practice

How a change goes from your team to your live site

Publishing a change safely means checking it, releasing it to the right environment, and knowing how to recover if something breaks. We can set up a repeatable process around the way your software is built and hosted today.

Put release steps in order

A release workflow can build and check a change before it is published. The team can see which steps passed and where a failure occurred.

Separate testing and live use

When appropriate, a test copy gives the team a place to review changes before customers see them. We agree which settings and data each environment needs.

Plan for recovery

We can set up useful alerts, backup checks, and a way to return to a known working release. The handover documents who acts when there is an issue.

What to share with us

Share where the software is hosted, how you publish changes now, and what makes a release difficult or risky for your team.

FAQ

Questions we hear about this service

Can you migrate my existing site without downtime?

Yes, we stage the new setup fully, test it, then switch DNS with effectively zero downtime. Rollback plans included.

Why Cloudflare specifically?

Best-in-class speed, security, and pricing for most business workloads. Where something else fits better, we'll recommend that instead.

Do we need DevOps as a small business?

You need the results: fast site, easy updates, backups. We set it up once, properly, then it mostly runs itself.

What about our existing domain and emails?

Handled, domains, DNS records, and email routing are migrated carefully with a checklist, not vibes.
