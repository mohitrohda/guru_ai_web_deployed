In practice

Choose a service by the problem you have

You can start with a business problem even if you have never commissioned software. Write down what people do now, what makes it hard, and what you want to change. The type of project usually becomes clearer once you have one example.

Customers cannot find or reach you

A website can explain your services and collect enquiries. If you already have a site, review where visitors get stuck before deciding what to rebuild.

Staff repeat work across tools

Automation can connect a repeatable task. Custom software may help if the team also needs a shared screen for requests, status, or decisions.

Customers need answers or self-service

An assistant can handle agreed questions. A portal or app can give signed-in users access to their own orders, documents, or appointments.
