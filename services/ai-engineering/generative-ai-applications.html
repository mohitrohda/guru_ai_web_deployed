The opportunity

Build a focused tool around generative AI.

Create an application that helps users draft, transform or review business content within a defined workflow. We design the product experience, model integration and review steps together. The aim is a useful tool with clear boundaries, rather than a prompt box disconnected from the way your team works.

Where it can fit

Useful starting points

  • Assisted business content drafting
  • Document summarisation and rewriting
  • Structured content transformation

In practice

Give your team a first draft to review

Generative AI can prepare a draft from instructions and selected material. For example, it can turn an approved set of product details into a draft description that staff can edit before publishing.

Choose a useful drafting task

We agree what the user will provide, which reference material may be used, and what a satisfactory draft should contain.

Make editing part of the screen

We can give staff a place to check the source, adjust the draft, and decide whether it is ready to use.

Test quality with examples

We compare outputs with examples your team considers acceptable and examine cases where the model invents or leaves out details.

What to share with us

Share the task, some examples of strong drafts, and anything that must be reviewed before it can go out.

Before we start

Questions worth answering.

Can it match our writing style?

We can use approved examples and guidelines to shape outputs, then evaluate them with your reviewers. Users should still be able to edit and approve the result.

Will our data be used for model training?

That depends on the provider, product configuration and agreement. We review your requirements and the applicable settings before selecting a service.

Can the app use several models?

Where it is useful, routing or provider abstraction can be included. We weigh the added complexity against quality, reliability and cost needs.

Can generated content be published automatically?

That can be technically possible, but the review and approval rules should reflect the consequences of errors. We define those boundaries before enabling external actions.

How do you manage model cost?

We can track usage, test prompt and context size, choose appropriate models and define rate or usage limits. Provider charges remain part of the operating budget.
