The opportunity

Keep the systems your business relies on in good shape.

Give your website or application a clear maintenance process after launch. We first assess the existing code, hosting and dependencies, then define what ongoing support includes. Updates, incident handling and small improvements are managed through an agreed scope and release process.

In practice

Make ongoing updates easier to review

Maintenance is the recurring work of checking, updating, and fixing a site or application. For example, a team can try an update in a test copy, check the important forms, and record what was changed before publishing.

Agree what to maintain

We review the software and discuss which updates, monitoring, and fixes the service should cover.

Plan and test changes

We can prioritise work, check affected areas, and set a release time that fits how your business uses the system.

Keep track of issues and updates

Your team can see what was reported, what changed, and which decisions still need approval.

What to share with us

Bring the current site or application details, known issues, and the tasks that need to keep working during updates.

Before we start

Questions worth answering.

Can you maintain a site built by another developer?

Potentially. We assess the technology, source access, hosting and condition of the code before agreeing support.

Is round-the-clock support included?

Coverage hours, response targets and escalation arrangements are defined in the support agreement; they are not assumed to be 24/7.

Are new features included?

Small changes may be included in an agreed allowance. Larger features or redesigns are scoped separately so expectations remain clear.

Does maintenance include hosting and domain fees?

Not automatically. Infrastructure and third-party subscriptions are itemised separately from the maintenance work unless the agreement explicitly includes them.

How are urgent requests handled?

The support agreement defines triage, escalation and response expectations. Urgency should be assessed against impact and the coverage you have agreed.
