In practice

Try a screen before paying to build it

A prototype is a clickable model of how a website or app could work. For example, a customer can try a sample booking flow and show where they expect different wording or another step before development begins.

Map the steps users take

We discuss what people come to do and what information they need at each point.

Design and link the screens

We can create screens and connect them to show the flow. This makes it easier to review choices about content, buttons, and layout.

Review on suitable devices

People can try the prototype on agreed screen sizes and give feedback. We capture decisions and prepare the designs for development.

Before we start

Questions worth answering.

Is a prototype a working application?

A clickable prototype demonstrates intended interactions. It does not normally include a production backend, real integrations or complete application logic.

Can you improve only one part of our product?

Yes. We can focus on a defined journey such as onboarding, checkout or a dashboard, while working within your existing design system.

Do you consider accessibility?

Accessibility considerations such as contrast, keyboard flow, labels and interaction states are included in design review. The implemented product still needs appropriate testing.

Can you work within our existing branding?

Yes. We can use your current brand guidelines and components, identify inconsistencies and suggest changes where they improve the user journey.

How do you gather feedback on a prototype?

We can define representative tasks, observe how reviewers move through them and record specific confusion or missing information. The review method depends on access to users and the project scope.
