The opportunity

Turn company knowledge into useful answers.

Help customers or employees find answers across approved information without searching through folders or asking the same questions repeatedly. We design retrieval and answer workflows around your content, users and permissions. The assistant can show its sources and hand questions to a person when the available material does not support an answer.

Where it can fit

Useful starting points

  • Employee policy and onboarding questions
  • Product manuals and internal support
  • Customer-facing knowledge libraries

In practice

Ask a question and check the source

An AI knowledge assistant searches documents you allow it to use and puts the relevant information into an answer. For example, a staff member can ask how to return a damaged item and open the policy passage cited in the reply.

Choose the source material

We organise agreed documents such as policies or manuals and check who is allowed to see each set.

Show where the answer came from

The interface can display references so a reader can check wording, dates, and details in the original.

Handle missing information

We can define what the assistant should do when the material is unclear, out of date, or outside the question's scope.

What to share with us

Bring a small set of current documents and typical questions. Include one that should go to an expert instead of getting an automatic answer.

People operations

Make internal guidance easier to find

An employee asks a policy question in everyday language. The assistant searches the approved handbook and returns a concise answer alongside the relevant source passages.

Design considerationPolicies stay under the control of their owners. Questions about individual circumstances can be routed to the people team instead of being treated as a final decision.

Find the relevant part of a manual

A support colleague describes a product issue and retrieves related instructions from the correct manual version. The interface can make product selection and source dates visible.

Design considerationThis is useful where information is spread across manuals or internal notes. Coverage and permission checks matter as much as the quality of the answer.

Before we start

Questions worth answering.

Can the assistant use our private documents?

It can be designed for private sources with agreed access controls. We first review document sensitivity, hosting requirements and model-provider settings before choosing the architecture.

Will it always give the correct answer?

No AI assistant can guarantee that. Source references, representative testing, uncertainty handling and human handover help make its limits visible.

How is company information updated?

We agree which sources are authoritative and whether updates should be manual, scheduled or triggered by a connected system.

Is this different from a general chatbot?

Yes. The focus is retrieving information from a defined, maintained source collection, with references and access rules. General conversation is secondary to helping users find supported answers.

What happens when documents contradict each other?

We can surface the conflict or avoid a definitive answer, but the authoritative source should be resolved by a content owner. A source governance process is part of making the assistant useful.
