Overview

What this service really means for you

Stock problems are rarely about the product, they are about not knowing the real numbers in time. We build inventory systems that track stock as it moves, not as someone remembers to update a spreadsheet at the end of the week.

Whether stock sits in one store or across several warehouses, owners get one accurate number instead of several conflicting ones.

In practice

Track a product from delivery to dispatch

An inventory system records how much stock you have and where it is held. If ten items arrive, two move to another location, and one is sold, each movement can be recorded with its date and reason.

A list of items and locations

Set up products, units, variants, and storage locations so staff use the same names when recording stock.

Every movement recorded

Log receipts, sales, transfers, and adjustments. We can include checks for situations such as damaged or missing items.

Stock views for the owner

We can show available quantities, items below an agreed level, and the movement history behind those numbers.

What to share with us

Bring an item list and examples of a receipt, transfer, or adjustment. Tell us how staff count stock today.

Outcomes

FAQ

Questions we hear about this service

Can this work alongside our existing billing software?

Yes. Inventory can be built standalone or connected with billing and POS systems we build, so stock updates automatically with every sale.

Do we need barcode scanners to use this?

No, scanners speed things up but are not required. Manual entry works too, scanning is an option, not a requirement.

Can it handle multiple warehouses or store locations?

Yes, stock is tracked per location with a combined view across all of them.

What does it cost?
