Overview

What this service really means for you

WhatsApp is great for a quick reply, terrible for tracking fifty open issues across three support staff. We build help desk systems that turn every customer issue into a ticket with a status, an owner, and a history, so nothing quietly gets forgotten.

Customers get a clear channel to raise issues. Your team gets a queue they can actually manage instead of scrolling through old chats trying to remember who promised what.

In practice

Give each support request a clear owner

A support ticket is a record of a customer issue and its progress. When a customer follows up, the next staff member can open the ticket, read the earlier discussion, and continue from the last decision.

Capture the request

We can set up a place for staff or customers to record the problem and the details needed to investigate it.

Assign and track it

Use clear stages, priorities, and owners. The team can see what is waiting for a customer, an internal check, or a final reply.

Review recurring problems

Reports can show unanswered requests or common types of issues, with links to the records behind each total.

What to share with us

Share a few typical requests, which channels they arrive through, and how the team decides who should handle them.

FAQ

Questions we hear about this service

Can customers still reach us through WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp can remain a channel, with messages logged as tickets so nothing gets lost outside the system.

How is this different from a shared email inbox?

A shared inbox has no status, no priority, and no ownership. Tickets make it clear what is open, who owns it, and what happened.

Can it track performance across multiple support staff?

Yes, response times and resolution rates are tracked per agent and as a team.

What does it cost?
