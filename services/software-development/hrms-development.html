Overview

What this service really means for you

Most HR software fails for a simple reason: it tries to do everything at once and ends up doing nothing well. We build HRMS systems around the handful of things that actually matter day to day, who is present, who is on leave, what everyone is owed at month end, and where an employee's documents actually live.

Owners get a clear view across every branch or location. Employees get a simple way to check their own leave balance and payslip without asking HR for it. Nothing more complicated than it needs to be.

In practice

From an attendance entry to an approved payroll record

A human resources management system, or HRMS, keeps employee information and routine work such as leave and attendance in one place. A staff member can request leave, their manager can review it, and the team can see the decision when preparing the next pay period.

Employee records

Store the details staff and managers need and restrict sensitive fields to the right roles.

Attendance and leave

Record attendance and requests against your schedules, leave rules, and approvals.

Payroll preparation

We can organise agreed inputs such as attendance, approved leave, or adjustments for payroll review and reporting.

What to share with us

Share the rules you already use, a sample attendance report without personal data, and who should approve exceptions.

FAQ

Questions we hear about this service

How is this different from a payroll only tool?

Payroll only tools stop at salary calculation. We build attendance, leave, documents, and payroll together, so the numbers feeding into payroll are already accurate.

Can it handle multiple branches or locations?

Yes. Attendance and leave are tracked per branch, with a combined view for owners and a branch level view for local managers.

Do employees need training to use it?

Most employees only need the self service screen, checking leave balance and payslips, which takes a few minutes to learn.

What does it cost?
