### Detailed Findings by Severity

#### [P1] Contrast Violation on 404 Button
- **Location:** `404.html` (`a.btn`)
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **Impact:** Fails WCAG AA (requires 4.5:1 for normal body/action text). Low contrast creates readability barriers for vision-impaired users.
- **WCAG/Standard:** WCAG 2.1 AA (SC 1.4.3 Contrast Minimum)
- **Recommendation:** Deepen the button gradient background or use primary brand blue `#1E50D8` with white text to achieve > 4.5:1.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

#### [P1] Sub-11px Functional Text and Tap Targets
- **Location:** `about.html`, `index.html`, `services/*.html` (eyebrow chips, service badges, meta tags)
- **Category:** Accessibility / Responsive Design
- **Impact:** Micro-text (measured down to 8.8px and 9.28px) degrades legibility on high-DPI mobile screens and violates minimum comfortable tap/read targets.
- **WCAG/Standard:** WCAG 2.1 AA (SC 1.4.4 Resize Text & SC 2.5.8 Target Size)
- **Recommendation:** Set a hard floor of 12px (min 11px for secondary metadata) on all functional text.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`

#### [P1] Infinite Decorative Motion & Continuous Loops
- **Location:** `style.css` (`.loader__beam::after`, `.showcase::after`), `index.html` (`.hero-live__pulse`, `.industry-tile__dot`)
- **Category:** Performance / Accessibility
- **Impact:** Continuously consumes GPU/CPU cycles on mobile devices and distracts users with synthetic motion that does not represent live data changes.
- **WCAG/Standard:** WCAG 2.1 AA (SC 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide)
- **Recommendation:** Restrict motion to user-triggered states or subtle, non-looping transitions.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable quieter`

#### [P2] Overused AI Archetype Heading Kickers & Eyebrow Chips
- **Location:** `about.html`, `index.html`, `services/*.html`
- **Category:** Implementation Integrity
- **Impact:** Dilutes brand authority by adopting the generic template aesthetic of generative AI builders.
- **Recommendation:** Integrate kickers into clear, compelling headings or transform them into functional breadcrumb navigation.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

#### [P2] Numbered Section Labels ("01 / Our story", "02 / ...")
- **Location:** `about.html`, `index.html`
- **Category:** Implementation Integrity
- **Impact:** Editorial scaffolding that acts as artificial structure instead of letting visual rhythm and typography guide the reader.
- **Recommendation:** Remove artificial numbering and rely on typographic scale and whitespace.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

#### [P2] Cramped Container Insets in Navigation & Panels
- **Location:** `style.css`, `.site-nav__services-panel`, `.ab-section`
- **Category:** Responsive Design / Layout
- **Impact:** Content text lands uncomfortably close to outer container bounds on narrow viewports.
- **Recommendation:** Enforce consistent 16px–24px internal container padding across mobile breakpoints.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

#### [P2] Residual Colored Glow Shadows
- **Location:** `style.css`, `404.html`
- **Category:** Theming
- **Impact:** High-saturation blue halos (`#2f6bff` box-shadows) create a dated cyberpunk halo aesthetic rather than crisp corporate depth.
- **Recommendation:** Replace chromatic box-shadow halos with multi-layered neutral elevation shadows.

- **Impact:** Letter spacing above 0.05em on multi-line body text slows comprehension and creates jagged reading flow.
- **Recommendation:** Confine letter-tracking (`0.05em+`) strictly to short uppercase micro-labels; keep body copy tracking neutral (`0` to `0.01em`).
