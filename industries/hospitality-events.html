Overview

Guests notice the moment something slows down

Hotels, restaurants, and event venues share one pressure point: the moment a guest is waiting, whether for a room to be ready, an order to arrive, or a booking to be confirmed. A room double booked, a kitchen order lost between table and counter, or an event date with no record of the advance paid all turn into a guest who notices.

We build billing and booking systems around that pressure, real time room availability for hotels, table wise ordering for restaurants, and a clear booking calendar with advance payment tracking for event venues, so staff can focus on the guest instead of the paperwork.

FAQ

Questions we hear from hotels, restaurants, and event venues

Can it show real time room availability for a hotel?

Yes. Real time room availability, check in and check out billing, and clear folio tracking are built in for small hotels and lodges.

Does it handle table wise ordering for a busy restaurant?

Yes. Table wise ordering, kitchen order tickets, and fast billing keep orders moving from table to kitchen to bill without confusion during the rush.

Can it track advance payments for event bookings?

Yes. A clear booking calendar, advance payment tracking, and guest count based billing are built for banquet halls and event venues.

Can the AI chatbot handle booking enquiries after hours?

Yes. The chatbot answers questions and captures enquiries at any hour, then hands off to a real person on WhatsApp when needed.
