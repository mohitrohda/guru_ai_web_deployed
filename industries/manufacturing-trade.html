Overview

Complexity that has to be tracked, not hidden

Manufacturers, distributors, and workshops deal with a different kind of complexity: raw material that moves through several production stages, bulk orders priced by quantity, and compliance paperwork that has to match what actually happened on the floor.

We build systems that track that complexity instead of hiding it, from an internal ERP that connects inventory and production, to billing software scoped to exactly how a garage, wholesaler, or manufacturer actually invoices.

FAQ

Questions we hear from manufacturers and distributors

Can it track raw material through multiple production stages?

Yes. Raw material tracking, production stages, and GST billing are connected in one system, the same approach we built for Accusize Gauges and Tools.

Does it support tiered pricing for bulk or wholesale orders?

Yes. Tiered pricing by quantity, bulk order billing, and dispatch tracking are built in for wholesalers and distributors who move high volumes.

We already have an ERP, can you extend it instead of replacing it?

Usually yes. We review what you have first and extend or integrate where it makes sense, rather than forcing a rebuild you do not need.

Can it handle job card billing for a garage alongside manufacturing work?

Yes. Job card tracking with spare parts and labour billed together is a separate, focused build, so a workshop gets exactly what it needs without carrying manufacturing complexity it does not.
