Different counters, the same need for speed and accuracy

Retail, fashion, and jewellery businesses live and die by what is on the shelf and what the counter can process in a rush. A multi branch fashion chain needs stock visibility across locations, a cloth store needs size and colour level tracking, and a jewellery counter needs live gold rate billing that cannot afford a single mistake.

We have built exactly this: multi branch stock visibility and consistent billing for MAAAX Men's Hub, a multi branch fashion retail chain in Pune, and a full business website for Rangrez, a premium cloth and fashion store in Maharashtra. The same thinking carries across to jewellers and textile stores with their own specific needs.

Questions we hear from retail, fashion, and jewellery businesses

Can it handle multiple branches with shared stock visibility?

Yes. Multi branch stock visibility and consistent billing across locations is exactly what we built for MAAAX Men's Hub, a multi branch fashion retail chain in Pune.

Does it track size, colour, and variant level stock?

Yes. Size, colour, and variant level stock tracking with fast counter billing is built in, the same approach behind the Rangrez cloth store build.

Can it handle live gold and silver rate billing for jewellery?

Yes. Live gold and silver rate billing with making charges and wastage percentage built in is a dedicated part of the jewellery billing software.

Do you also build the website, or only the billing software?

Both, if you want them. Many retail and fashion clients start with a website, then add billing once they are ready, or the other way around.
