Overview

A business built on appointments and repeat trust

Salons, spas, and fitness studios run on appointments, repeat visits, and trust built one client at a time. The common failure points are familiar: a stylist double booked during a busy Saturday, a membership that quietly lapsed without a reminder, commission that takes an evening to calculate by hand.

We build software and AI tools around that reality. Billing that keeps up with a packed floor, a chatbot that answers booking questions after closing time, and a website that makes a first time visitor comfortable enough to walk in.

FAQ

Questions we hear from salons, spas, and studios

Can it handle multiple stylists or therapists billing at the same time?

Yes. Multiple counters can bill at once with role based access for staff, managers, and owners, the same system we already run for multi chair salons.

Does it track membership expiry and renewals for gyms?

Yes. Membership billing comes with automatic renewal reminders and attendance tracking, so members stay active and your front desk stops chasing payments manually.

Can the AI chatbot take bookings outside business hours?

Yes. The chatbot answers questions and guides customers toward booking at any hour, even late at night, and hands off to a real person on WhatsApp when needed.

Do you build a website alongside the billing software, or only one or the other?

Either. Most salon and fitness clients start with a website and chatbot, then add billing once they are ready, or the other way around. Each piece works on its own and together.
