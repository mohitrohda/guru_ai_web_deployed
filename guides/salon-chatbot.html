What should a salon chatbot answer?

A salon assistant can reply to common questions while the front desk is busy. Give it a short list of jobs to do and make it clear when a team member needs to take over.

By GURU AI Technologies, Pune · Updated 6 November 2026

Answer questions that have a reliable source

Start with the questions staff answer repeatedly: where you are, which services you offer, when you open, and what the published prices cover. Collect those answers in one place so someone on the team can update them when hours or services change.

If a service price depends on a consultation, the assistant can explain that and share the next step. It should not invent a price or promise that a service suits someone it has not seen.

Decide what counts as a booking

A customer asking for a haircut tomorrow afternoon is an enquiry. The assistant can collect a preferred time and send the conversation to the front desk. A booking is confirmed only after an available slot has been selected in the system the salon uses.

Our booking demo shows the connected version: a message arrives, the details are captured, a lead is saved, and a confirmation is sent after an available time is chosen. A salon without a shared calendar can start with a WhatsApp handover instead.

Give staff a clear handover

Send uncertain questions, special requests, and changes to an existing booking to a person. Tell the customer when this has happened. Pass along the service, preferred time, and what the customer has already shared so the team does not need to start over.

In our salon website and chatbot project, MR.COOL answered common service and pricing questions and helped visitors move toward a conversation on WhatsApp. That is a practical first step even when the team still confirms appointments itself.

How clinic enquiries differ

A clinic assistant can share opening hours, location and appointment options. Send questions about symptoms, treatment, or test results to the clinic’s team. For a booking request, collect only the information your staff needs to arrange the appointment and keep the handover private.

Test the awkward questions as well as the easy ones

Try a fully booked day, a price that depends on the service, and a customer who wants to change their time. Use the languages customers actually type. Confirm that the assistant gets the customer to the right person when the available information is not enough.
